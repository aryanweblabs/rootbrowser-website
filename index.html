Frequently Asked

Common questions about RootBrowser

Yes! RootBrowser is 100% open source under the MIT License. You can review the entire codebase on GitHub and compile it yourself.

RootBrowser includes built-in Tor support. When you enable Private Mode, all traffic is routed through Tor's 3-hop circuits. You'll also get enhanced fingerprint protection and in-memory-only cookies.

RootBrowser requires Qt 6.2+ and a modern C++ compiler (GCC 9+, Clang 10+, or MSVC 2019+). It runs on Linux, Windows, and macOS. Check the build instructions for your OS.

RootBrowser focuses on privacy-first design with built-in Tor, fingerprint protection, and a clean, modern UI. We use Chromium for rendering (like Chrome) but with privacy as the default, not an afterthought.

Bookmarks and history are stored locally in JSON format on your machine. Private mode doesn't store anything—cookies and cache are kept in RAM only and cleared when you close the window.

RootBrowser has a built-in ad blocker that works on YouTube and other sites. Extension support depends on your compiled version, but the core browser includes privacy tools by default.
